============ SPRINT ============
05 The 15-day sprint

The rhythm of the sprint

Three weeks of five teaching days. Most days move through the same sequence of sessions — worth reading once before the full plan, so the table below can be scanned for what changes day to day.

The daily sequence

On TED talk days and game days alike, a day runs through these sessions in order. Game days open with a game and its debrief in place of the talk; everything after is shared.

Media / Game → Question Generation → Commit to Initial Positions → Self-Directed Research → Revise Positions → Discussion (Small Group / Fishbowl) → Full Class Discussion → Written Reflections → Digital Portfolio & Office Hours
